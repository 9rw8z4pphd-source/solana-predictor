How The Prediction Engine Works

This tool runs 7 independent signals on live Solana market data fetched from CoinGecko and Alternative.me. Each signal analyses a different dimension of the market — price momentum, trading volume, market sentiment, Bitcoin correlation, technical strength (RSI), 7-day trend direction, and price volatility. Every signal casts a vote: bullish, bearish, or neutral. The consensus engine counts the votes, measures agreement strength, and generates a confidence-weighted prediction with a plain-English explanation of why the signals agree or disagree. Data refreshes every 60 seconds. This is for learning and experimentation only — not financial advice.

Momentum

Momentum tracks whether price is accelerating upward or downward. A strong positive change means buyers are actively pushing price higher. A strong negative change means sellers are in control. Signals above +3% are bullish, below -3% are bearish. Everything between is neutral territory.

Volume

Volume measures how much SOL has been traded in the last 24 hours compared to a typical quiet day ($2.5B baseline). When volume spikes above 150% of baseline, it signals unusual market activity — often preceding or confirming a significant move. Very low volume suggests a quiet, directionless market.

The Fear & Greed Index scores the entire crypto market's emotional state from 0 (extreme fear — people are panic selling) to 100 (extreme greed — people are euphoric and overbying). This signal is contrarian: extreme fear is often a buying opportunity, extreme greed often precedes a correction.

BTC Correlation

Following Bitcoin's lead

--

SOL and Bitcoin move in the same direction roughly 70% of the time. When Bitcoin rises strongly, altcoins like SOL typically follow within hours. When Bitcoin drops hard, it drags the whole market down. This signal uses BTC's 24h change as a directional hint for SOL's near-term movement.

📈

RSI

Overbought or oversold?

--
Loading...

--

RSI (Relative Strength Index) is a classic technical indicator scored 0–100. Above 70 means SOL has been bought heavily and may be due for a pullback (overbought). Below 30 means it has been sold heavily and may bounce back (oversold). Between 30 and 70 is the neutral zone where no strong signal exists.

7D Trend

This signal compares today's price against the 7-day average price. If SOL is trading significantly above its weekly average, it suggests upward momentum over a longer timeframe. If it's below the weekly average, it suggests a broader downtrend. This filters out short-term noise.

🌪️

Volatility

How wildly is price swinging?

Volatility measures the standard deviation of daily price changes over 7 days. High volatility means the price is swinging wildly — predictions become less reliable and risk is higher. Low volatility means the market is calm and consolidating, often before a breakout in either direction. This signal votes neutral when volatility is extreme, as direction becomes impossible to predict.

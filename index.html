How This Works

This tool runs 5 independent prediction signals on live Solana data. Each signal analyses a different aspect of the market — price momentum, trading volume, market sentiment, Bitcoin correlation, and technical strength. They then vote on whether SOL is likely to move up, down, or sideways. The more signals that agree, the higher the confidence score. This is for learning and experimentation only — not financial advice.
